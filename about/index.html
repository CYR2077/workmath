About Workmath

A small, independent set of calculators for the arithmetic of working life.

What this site is

Workmath is a collection of free calculators covering pay, hours, and personal targets. Each one does a single job properly: overtime pay and its premium, the conversion between an hourly wage and a salary, splitting a tip pool between people who worked different hours, accumulated sleep debt, the inflation adjustment that a savings goal needs, and the hourly rate a freelancer actually has to charge.

Every calculator runs entirely in the browser. There is no account, no upload, no server-side processing of the figures you enter, and no analytics product that records them.

Why these tools exist

The arithmetic itself is not hard. What is hard is knowing which arithmetic to do. Splitting a tip pool by hours is trivial once you know the pool total and who worked what — the difficulty is agreeing on the method before anyone goes home. Converting an hourly wage to a salary is one multiplication, but the number that matters is the one divided by the hours you actually work, not the 2,080 a payroll system assumes.

Most calculators online hide their assumptions inside a black box and print a confident number. These pages do the opposite: the formula is printed on the page, worked through with real figures, and the parts of the model that are simplifications are labelled as such.

How the numbers are produced

  • Plain JavaScript, no dependencies. The page you load is the whole program. Nothing is sent anywhere to be calculated.
  • Formulas published. Each calculator page shows the formula and a worked example, so the result can be checked by hand.
  • Rules cited, not invented. Where a figure depends on law — the federal 40-hour overtime threshold, state daily-overtime rules, tip-pool participation rules — the page states the rule and links the reasoning, then lets you override the parameter instead of baking in a guess.
  • Corrections welcome. If a rule has changed or an assumption is wrong, the contact page is the fastest route and corrections are usually live within a few days.

What this site is not

These are planning tools, not professional advice. Nothing here is tax advice, legal advice, financial advice, or medical advice. Employment rules differ between countries and between states within a country, and they change. A calculator cannot read your contract, your payslip, or your local statute. Where a decision has money or health consequences, treat the output as a sanity check on your own reasoning and confirm the details with your employer, a labour authority, an accountant, or a clinician.

How the site is funded

Workmath is free and supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Ads are the only reason the site needs to exist commercially; they do not affect the calculators and they cannot see the numbers you type into them. See the privacy policy for exactly what is collected and how to opt out.

Corrections and contact

If you find a calculation that is wrong, a rule that has changed, or a page that does not work on your device, please say so. Reports that include the numbers you entered and the result you expected are fixed fastest. Use the contact page.
