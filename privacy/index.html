Privacy Policy

Short version: the numbers you type into a calculator never leave your browser. The pages themselves are served with advertising, and this policy explains exactly what that involves.

Effective 18 November 2026 · Last updated 18 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Workmath ("we", "us") operates this website. For any question about this policy or about data handled by the site, contact privacy@workmath.net.

2. What the calculators do with your data

Nothing. Every calculator on this site is a static page containing JavaScript that performs the arithmetic locally, inside your browser tab. The figures you enter — wages, hours, savings balances, sleep times — are not transmitted to us, not stored on a server, and not logged. Closing the tab discards them.

There is no account system, no login, and no server-side component that receives calculator input. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet after the page has loaded: the calculators continue to work.

3. Information stored in your browser

One item is saved locally, using your browser's localStorage: your currency preference, so that the symbol you selected persists between visits. It contains a short currency code such as USD and nothing else. It stays on your device, is not readable by us, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

If you give consent through the advertising consent message, the advertising provider may also store cookies as described in section 5.

4. Server logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data — IP address, requested URL, referrer, user agent, and timestamp — in order to deliver the page and to defend against abuse and denial-of-service traffic. This data is aggregated and retained for a limited period for security and performance purposes. We do not use it to build profiles of individual visitors and we do not combine it with anything you enter into a calculator.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-preserving measurement service that does not use cookies or fingerprinting and does not track individuals across sites. It reports aggregate figures such as page views and referrers.

5. Advertising

This site displays advertising supplied by Google AdSense, which is how the calculators remain free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks may also place or read cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies on this site to measure ad performance and to serve relevant advertising.
  • Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your consent choice and your region. Where consent is required — for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK — personalised advertising cookies are only set after you agree.

How to control advertising cookies

  • Opt out of personalised advertising by Google: google.com/settings/ads
  • Opt out of participating third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising: aboutads.info/choices (US) or youronlinechoices.com (EU/UK)
  • Google's partner sites policy and the list of vendors it works with: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Change or withdraw your consent at any time using the privacy settings link in the footer, where a consent message is shown in your region.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Note that blocking all cookies does not remove ads — it makes them non-personalised.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share calculator inputs with advertisers, because we never receive them.

6. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising cookies and any associated storage or access to information on your device. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — for serving pages, maintaining security, preventing abuse, and measuring aggregate traffic in a privacy-preserving way. These interests do not override your rights, and we minimise the data involved.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access the personal data held about you; correct inaccurate data; erase data; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw consent. Because the site does not collect the figures you enter, most requests will concern cookie-based advertising data, which is controlled by the advertising vendor — the opt-out links in section 5 are the fastest route.

To exercise any right with respect to this site, email privacy@workmath.net. We respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

8. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies set by third-party vendors may constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA; you can opt out through the links in section 5 or through a Global Privacy Control signal, which participating vendors honour. We do not knowingly collect personal information from consumers under 16. Categories of information that may be processed by third-party advertising vendors include identifiers (cookie IDs) and internet activity (pages visited). This information is used to serve and measure advertising, and is retained according to the vendor's own policy.

9. International transfers

The site is served through a global content delivery network, so a request may be handled by a server outside your country. Advertising vendors may process data in the United States and elsewhere. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent safeguard operated by the vendor.

10. Children

This site is intended for adults managing their own pay and budgets. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to an advertising vendor through this site, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

11. Data retention

We retain no calculator data. Server logs held by the hosting provider are retained for a short security window and then discarded. Advertising data retention is governed by the vendor's policy, linked in section 5.

12. Security

The site is served over HTTPS with modern TLS. Because there is no account system and no database of user input, the personal data at risk from a breach of this site is limited to standard request metadata. No system is perfectly secure, and we make no absolute guarantee.

13. Links to other sites

Pages may link to external resources such as government labour pages or tax authorities. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this one does not apply to them.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — for example if a new advertising partner or analytics service is introduced — the revised version will be published here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: privacy@workmath.net, or use the contact page.
