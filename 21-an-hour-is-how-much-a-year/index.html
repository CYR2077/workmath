$21 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

Forty hours a week at $21.00 an hour is $43,680 a year before tax — about $3,640 a month, or $1,680 per biweekly paycheque. The tables below break it down by pay period and by how many hours you actually work.

How the figure is calculated

annual = rate × hours per week × weeks per year
$21.00 × 40 × 52 = $43,680

The 40 × 52 baseline is 2,080 hours, which is what payroll systems and lenders use. It assumes you are paid for every week of the year, including public holidays and paid vacation. Any unpaid week off lowers it: two weeks unpaid at $21 an hour is $1,680 less.

What comes out before it reaches you

On $43,680 of gross wages, the deductions that apply to almost every US employee are Social Security at 6.2% and Medicare at 1.45% — 7.65% combined, or $3,342 a year at this rate. That part is not optional and does not vary by state.

Federal income tax is the variable one. It depends on your filing status, other household income, and the standard deduction for the year. A single filer earning $43,680 takes the standard deduction and is taxed on what remains in graduated brackets starting at 10%, so the effective federal rate lands well below the top bracket they reach. State income tax ranges from nothing in nine states to over 9% in the highest-tax states, and benefits deductions — health insurance, retirement contributions — come out on top of that.

A realistic planning range for take-home pay at this rate is $34,070 to $38,438 before state tax. Use your own payslip for the real number; the range is for budgeting, not for filing.

Overtime changes the picture more than the rate does

At $21.00 an hour, overtime is paid at $31.50. Five extra hours a week — a 45-hour week — takes the annual figure from $43,680 to $51,870, an increase of $8,190. That is worth more than a $2 an hour raise at 40 hours.

Note that not every salaried role is exempt from overtime, and not every hourly role gets it. Federal law requires 1.5× for hours over 40 in a workweek for non-exempt employees; some states add a daily threshold. Run your actual hours through the overtime calculator if the number matters for a decision.

What $21 an hour means in practice

Twenty-one dollars an hour is a typical rate for a technician with one or two years of experience — HVAC assistant, maintenance tech, phlebotomist with a few years in. These roles tend to have clearer progression than pure service work.

Comfortable in mid-cost areas and reasonable in expensive ones if you have no dependants. The presence of a defined promotion ladder matters more here than the number itself.

Frequently asked questions

$21 an hour is how much a year?

$43,680 before tax at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. That is $3,640 a month gross, $1,680 per biweekly paycheque, and $840 a week.

$21 an hour is how much a year after tax?

Social Security and Medicare take $3,342 of that. Federal income tax depends on filing status and the standard deduction; state tax depends on where you live. Expect roughly $34,070 to $38,438 in take-home pay before state tax.

How much is $21 an hour biweekly?

$1,680 gross for 80 hours. With five hours of overtime in the period, add $157.50.

What if I work 50 hours a week at $21 an hour?

The first 40 hours pay $840; the 10 overtime hours pay $31.50 each, or $315. That is $1,155.00 a week and $60,060 a year, assuming the overtime is paid as federal law requires.

Is $21 an hour good pay?

Comfortable in mid-cost areas and reasonable in expensive ones if you have no dependants. The presence of a defined promotion ladder matters more here than the number itself.

How do I compare $21 an hour to a salary offer?

Convert the salary to an effective hourly rate by dividing it by the hours you will really work, not by 2,080. A $44k salary on a genuine 45-hour week is $19.41 an hour over 50 working weeks — compare that against $21.00 with guaranteed hours.
