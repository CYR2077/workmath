Typical response time is two to three working days. Messages about a broken page or a wrong figure are answered first.

What to include in a bug report

  1. Which calculator — the page name or the URL.
  2. The inputs — every number you entered, in order.
  3. The result you saw and the result you expected.
  4. Your reasoning — the formula or rule that makes the current output wrong. This is the part that turns a report into a fix.
  5. Device and browser, if the problem is visual or does not appear on a desktop.

Rules and rates that have changed

Employment law, minimum wages, and tax rates change, and a page that was right last year can be wrong this year. If you can point to the current statute, government page, or official guidance, the correction will be made and the page's review date updated. Please include the link.

Advertising and privacy

For questions about the ads on this site or about data handling, see the privacy policy first — it covers what Google AdSense collects, the cookie choices available to you, and how to withdraw consent. If something there is unclear, email the address above and say which section.

What we cannot help with

This site cannot give individual tax, legal, or medical advice, calculate your specific payslip, or intervene in a dispute with an employer. For unpaid wages, a labour authority or an employment lawyer in your jurisdiction is the right route; for a persistent sleep problem, a clinician is.
